Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Draft: this document is a working draft pending legal review and has not been finalized. Do not treat it as binding until reviewed and published.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ciudadano Ready ("we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: when you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text) to create and secure your account.

Contact form submissions: if you message us through the Contact page, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content.

Course progress: we store which lessons and stages you've completed so your dashboard reflects your progress.

Language preference: your choice of English or Spanish is remembered in your browser's local storage; it is not sent to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide and improve the Service, respond to support and contact requests, secure your account, and communicate important updates about your account or the course.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted third parties to operate Ciudadano Ready:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and handles account authentication.
  • Resend: delivers transactional email notifications (for example, alerting our team when you submit the contact form).

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to remember your language preference and session. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies.

5. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Contact form messages are retained to maintain a record of support requests. You may request deletion of your data as described below.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted password storage and row-level access controls, so users can only access their own account data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Ciudadano Ready is intended for adult naturalization applicants and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

8. Your Choices

You can review and update your account information at any time, or request that we delete your account and associated data, by contacting us through the Contact page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us via the Contact page.
