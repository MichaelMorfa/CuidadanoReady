A SIMULATED CITIZENSHIP INTERVIEW

Practice the real thing.

You'll be presented with one question at a time, each with a short video setting up the moment, just like an officer would ask it in person.

  • Watch the video, then answer in your own words, choose an answer, or respond yes/no, depending on the question.
  • After each answer, you'll see a short review before moving to the next question.
  • At the end, you'll get a summary of how the session went.
This is a practice exercise only. It is not a legal assessment of your eligibility for U.S. citizenship, and it does not predict the outcome of an actual USCIS interview.
