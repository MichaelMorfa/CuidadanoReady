Every stage of the naturalization process, broken into short lessons with real practice questions.

STAGE 1

A personal welcome from our co-founder, an immigration attorney, and an overview of what the course covers.

STAGE 2

Eligibility

STAGE 3

N-400 Application

Educational walkthrough of the N-400 so you understand the form, terminology, and questions you may encounter.
